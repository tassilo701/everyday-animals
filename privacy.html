Privacy

Everyday Animals · last updated 4 October 2026

The short version

Everyday Animals collects nothing. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no in-app chat. Children can use it safely without anything being sent anywhere.

What stays on the device

  • The app's own files (animal photos, sounds, videos, maps) so it works offline.
  • A few settings chosen in the grown-ups corner (sounds on/off, read-aloud, games, day/night look) and whether the zoom hint was already shown.

This data never leaves the device. Deleting the app (or clearing the website's data) removes it.

Location

The app does not ask for or use your location. It starts the globe on your part of the world using only the device's time-zone setting, which never leaves the device.

Read-aloud

Words are read aloud by the voice built into your device. Nothing is recorded and no microphone is used.

Internet use

The app downloads its own content (from the same place it is hosted) the first time it is opened, and when grown-ups choose "save everything for offline". It does not contact any other service. Links in the credits open the original photo or sound pages on Wikimedia Commons or iNaturalist only if a grown-up taps them.

Children

The app is made for children aged 2–15. It contains no advertising, no purchases inside the free app, no social features and no links outside the grown-ups corner.

Credits

Photos, sounds and videos are by naturalists on Wikimedia Commons and iNaturalist, under CC0, CC BY or CC BY-SA licences, credited in the app. Where animals live: GBIF.org. Borders: Natural Earth.
